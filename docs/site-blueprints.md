# Site Blueprints for 5 U.S. Local-Service Content Sites

## Document Purpose

This document defines differentiated content blueprints for five Astro monorepo sites targeting U.S. local service businesses.  
The goal is to avoid generic SEO content and build editorially distinct, conversion-aware properties that can attract operators, clinic owners, and marketing leads with practical, industry-specific guidance.

---

## 1) massage-growth

### Website Positioning
Practical growth playbook for independent massage therapists and small multi-room massage studios in the U.S., focused on client retention, package sales, and recurring revenue.

### Target Users
- Solo massage therapists transitioning from marketplace dependency to direct bookings
- Studio owners (2-10 therapists) trying to improve utilization and rebooking rates
- Front-desk or operations managers handling scheduling and promotions

### Visual Style
- Warm neutrals, soft earth tones, high-trust and calming
- Real treatment-room photography, subtle texture backgrounds
- Rounded UI components, “wellness journal” feel rather than corporate clinic style

### Homepage Structure
1. Hero: “Grow repeat clients without discount fatigue”
2. Revenue Snapshot Module: package mix, rebooking benchmarks, no-show cost calculator
3. Playbook Blocks by Goal: Fill weekdays / Improve rebook rate / Raise average ticket
4. Scripts Library Preview: consultation and checkout scripts
5. Mini Case Teasers: before-after metrics from real studio scenarios
6. Lead Magnet CTA: “60-day retention reset plan”

### Content Sections
- Pricing & Packages
- Retention Systems
- Intake & Client Experience
- Local Partnerships
- Operations & Front Desk Scripts
- Metrics & Dashboards

### First 10 Article Topics
1. How U.S. massage studios can structure 3-session intro packages without training discount-only clients
2. Rebooking at checkout: exact script framework for Swedish and deep-tissue appointments
3. Reducing no-shows in massage therapy with card-on-file and SMS timing rules (state-compliant patterns)
4. Designing a weekday “desk worker recovery” offer for downtown massage studios
5. Converting first-time prenatal massage clients into recurring care plans
6. Membership vs package model for small massage businesses: margin comparison by capacity level
7. Intake form upgrades that increase add-on acceptance (cupping, hot stone, aromatherapy)
8. Building referral loops with chiropractors and PT clinics for postural-care clients
9. How to fix therapist utilization gaps across morning, afternoon, and evening shifts
10. 30-day win-back workflow for lapsed massage clients (90+ day inactive list)

### 3 Template / Checklist Topics
- Massage Studio Rebooking Script Checklist (front desk + therapist handoff)
- New Client Retention Workflow Template (day 0, day 2, day 14, day 45 touchpoints)
- No-Show Prevention SOP Checklist for Appointment-Based Wellness Businesses

### 2 Case Breakdown Topics
- Case Breakdown: Growing a 3-room massage studio from 41% to 63% rebook rate in 12 weeks
- Case Breakdown: Replacing deep discounts with value-stacked packages and lifting average ticket by 22%

### 1 Data Report Topic
- 2026 U.S. Massage Studio Retention Benchmarks: rebook rates, package attach rates, and cancellation patterns

### Image Asset Recommendations
- Treatment rooms, therapist-client consultation moments, checkout desk interactions
- Visual charts for rebook funnel and package revenue mix

- “What is massage therapy” beginner explainers with no business angle

## Cross-Site Differentiation Guardrails

- Each site must maintain unique terminology, examples, and KPI emphasis by vertical.
- Homepage modules should not be cloned across sites; conversion path and content hierarchy must remain role-specific.
- Editorial calendar overlap should stay below 20% topic similarity in any quarter.
- Every article brief must include: target role, operational pain point, measurable outcome, and U.S. local-market context.
