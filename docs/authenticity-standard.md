# Massage Business Growth Authenticity Standard

## 1) Website Positioning

Massage Business Growth is not a generic SEO blog.  
It is a local marketing resource center built for U.S. massage clinics, independent massage therapists, and wellness studios.

Core positioning principles:

- Industry-specific: every page should reflect massage business realities
- Operator-focused: content should help owners and managers make practical growth decisions
- Local-market grounded: content should align with U.S. local search, Google Maps, reviews, and booking workflows
- Resource-center oriented: the site should feel like an evolving knowledge base, not a one-off content project

---

## 2) Authenticity Goals

After landing on the site, users should feel:

- This website deeply understands the massage industry
- Content is written for real massage business owners/operators
- The pages are not generic AI blog output
- Articles include concrete scenarios, templates, checklists, workflows, and case breakdowns
- The site has long-term editorial continuity and resource-center credibility

Authenticity test (pass/fail):

- Can a massage clinic owner immediately identify their day-to-day challenges on-page?
- Does the page include actionable business operations details?
- Would this content still be useful without SEO jargon?
- Does the page show editorial intent beyond keyword coverage?

---

## 3) Content That Must Not Appear

The following content is prohibited:

- `Hello, Astronaut`
- Astro default template copy
- Generic “What is SEO” explanations
- Generic “Why SEO is important” articles
- Generic “Top 10 SEO tips” list posts
- Vague marketing advice with no massage-specific context

Also avoid:

- Broad, keyword-stuffed content without operational relevance
- Inflated claims or formulaic AI phrasing
- Recycled local SEO intros with no clinic-level detail

---

## 4) Homepage Required Elements

The homepage must include:

- Clear massage-industry positioning in hero and opening message
- Four core growth directions:
  - Google Maps
  - Reviews
  - Booking conversion
  - Massage SEO
- Featured Resource block
- Templates and Breakdowns block
- Latest Guides block
- Clear, visible CTA
- Links to `About` and `Editorial Policy`

Homepage quality checks:

- A first-time visitor should understand who the site is for within 5 seconds
- Core navigation should map to real operator outcomes, not generic marketing categories
- CTA should feel service-oriented and trustworthy, not hype-driven

---

## 5) Article Required Standards

Every article must include:

- Massage-specific business scenarios
- Step-by-step operational actions
- A checklist section or template section
- FAQ section
- Natural expert tone (not generic AI voice)
- Realistic recommendations (no exaggerated promises)
- No ranking guarantees

Editorial requirements:

- Use owner/operator language: bookings, no-shows, rebooking, front desk workflow, local demand
- Provide practical implementation detail (who does what, when, and how often)
- Include clear scope and constraints where relevant
- Prefer decision frameworks over generic bullet lists

Prohibited claims:

- “Guaranteed rankings”
- “Guaranteed #1 on Google”
- “Instant growth without effort”

---

## 6) Image Asset Standard

Version 1 image strategy:

- Use custom SVG diagrams, workflow graphics, and checklist previews
- Use visual assets that reinforce practical implementation (systems, SOPs, conversion flow, KPI snapshots)
- Keep style consistent with a professional local-service resource center

Image authenticity guidelines:

- Every image should support a specific point in the article
- Avoid decorative stock imagery that adds no informational value
- Favor explanatory visuals over generic lifestyle visuals
- Use clear naming conventions and reusable asset structure for long-term editorial maintenance

---

## 7) Trust Page Standard

The following trust pages must be planned and maintained:

- `About`
- `Editorial Policy`
- `Contact`

Each trust page should clarify:

- Who the site serves and why it exists
- How content is created, reviewed, and updated
- How users can submit questions, corrections, or partnership inquiries

Trust system goal:

- Reduce ambiguity about authorship and intent
- Increase confidence that the site is actively maintained
- Reinforce that content is written for real local service operators, not mass-generated traffic content
